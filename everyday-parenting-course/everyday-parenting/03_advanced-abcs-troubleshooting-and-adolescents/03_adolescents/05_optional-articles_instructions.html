Guide your teenager through the high-risk years

Source: Kazdin, A.E., & Rotella, C. (2010, February 5). II: No brakes! The best way to guide your teenager through the high-risk years. Slate Magazine, http://www.slate.com/id/2243436/

Summary: While the tendency to engage in risky behavior is biological, you can reduce the likelihood by creating positive environmental factors such as monitoring, modeling values, developing competencies and interests, and fostering a healthy connection with your child (not too tight and not too loose).
