You may have noticed that the learning goals in this course often start with “practice,” “use,” or “build.” This language demonstrates that the objective is not just to learn the material, but to apply the knowledge in your home. This assignment will encourage you to practice the techniques to change your child’s behavior and make your typical day at home easier.

This peer review assignment assumes that you have practiced at least one of the parenting techniques in your home. If you have not tested out a technique from the course, please select any technique and try it out in your home for a week or two before starting the assignment. When you are ready, please take 15 minutes or so to answer the questions below.

Review criteria

The grading is primarily based on participation. There will be a few terms that need to be accurately identified and explained but otherwise this is a forum to share your experience practicing the parenting tools in your home.
