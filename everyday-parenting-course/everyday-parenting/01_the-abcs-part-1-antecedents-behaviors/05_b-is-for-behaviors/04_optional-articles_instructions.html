I Spy Daddy Giving Someone the Finger

Source: Kazdin, A.E., & Rotella, C. (2009, January 27). I Spy Daddy Giving Someone the Finger. Your kids will imitate you: Use it as a force for good. Slate Magazine, http://www.slate.com/id/2209882/
