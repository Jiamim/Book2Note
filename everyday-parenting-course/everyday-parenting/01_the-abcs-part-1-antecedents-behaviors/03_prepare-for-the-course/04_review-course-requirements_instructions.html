Review Course Requirements

Thank you again for enrolling in this course. While we would hope that you go through the entire course, we are certain that even watching a handful of lectures and practicing those strategies will make a significant change in the home.

The entire course is relatively short, with 4 weeks of material averaging around 3 hours a week accounting for videos, quizzes, and optional readings. If you only watch the lecture materials, that would take around 5 hours and 30 minutes total. Just think, if you traded a few nights of Netflix for this course, you could learn valuable behavior-change techniques and general strategies to drastically improve daily life with your children.

Please note that while there are assignments built into the course and programmed deadlines, they are optional. There is no required homework. You can even turn off deadline reminders in your settings .

However, if you do plan on passing the course and/or receiving a certificate, you can find an outline of the requirements below.

Assignments

The key to passing the course, and getting a certificate, is passing the 21 technique mastery quizzes and 1 peer-review assignment which asks you demonstrate knowledge of the ABC techniques and share your experience implementing the techniques in the home.

Grading

There is a graded quiz following each topic to reinforce the material and put your knowledge to the test. The quizzes are only a few questions and are weighted from 3-5% of your final grade. The Peer Review Assignment accounts for 27% of the final grade.

You must pass every graded assessment to pass the course, regardless of your final grade.

Deadlines

If you miss a deadline within the course, it won't affect your grade or ability to get a certificate. You just need to submit all assignments before the final deadline of the course. However, if you do fall behind, then you can transfer your existing progress by switching to a new session. Your progress will transfer into the new session without penalty.
