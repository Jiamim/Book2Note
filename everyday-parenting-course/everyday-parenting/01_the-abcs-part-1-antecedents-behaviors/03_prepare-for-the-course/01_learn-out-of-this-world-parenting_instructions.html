Learn Out of This World Parenting

In the ABCs portion of the course – Modules 1 and 2 – each lecture outlines several steps guiding you on how to implement the technique in your home. For example, in the first lesson video on Praise there are 4 Key Ingredients:

1. Select a behavior

2. Praise effusively

3. State the specific behavior

4. Add a gentle touch

You will see a cartoon family model each step in the process. One thing to note is that our illustrated family will not look like your family - not only because they are cartoons, but also because they are aliens.

We want your parenting to be out of this world and the animated aliens will be your model.

You can find PDFs and accessible PowerPoint presentations below the lecture video in the "Downloads" section to download and print the technique guides for handy reference.
