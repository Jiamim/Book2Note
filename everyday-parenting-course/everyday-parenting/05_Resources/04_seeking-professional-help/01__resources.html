Guidelines to Decide Whether Professional Help is Needed

1. Impairment in daily functioning

2. Behaviors that may be dangerous or risk of being dangerous to oneself or others

3. Unusual behaviors and signs that are well out of the ordinary

4. Stark changes in behavior or characteristics you have seen in the child

5. Signs of distress

6. Child is unmanageable at home or at school

Key Questions to Ask Before You Begin Treatment

Ask your service or treatment provider:

  • What is the treatment you provide for my child’s problems?

  • How long have you been providing this treatment?

  • Has this particular treatment been studied, and does it have scientific evidence in its favor?

  • What are treatment options other than the one you provide?
